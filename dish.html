A LITTLE MORE ABOUT IT

From our table to yours.

This is the pasta our chef Elias grew up making on Sundays: flour-dusted hands, a pot of tomatoes quietly bubbling, and plenty of time around the table. We make the pasta fresh each morning and let the sauce take its time. The result is a simple, generous dish that tastes a little like coming home.
